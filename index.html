LOOP documentation

overview

LOOP is a token launchpad on Solana where financial privacy is part of the launch, not an add-on. Every token created here supports confidential balances and private transfers through Solana Privacy Rings from its first block.

The token itself is an ordinary SPL token with a fixed supply of one billion. It trades on the market like any other and can be bought and sold anywhere on Solana. LOOP adds the option for every holder to keep their balance and transfer amounts encrypted.

privacy rings

A privacy ring is a shielded pool on Solana where balances are stored encrypted. A transfer inside it is a normal Solana transaction, and the network checks a zero-knowledge proof that the transfer is valid without seeing the balances involved.

The owner of a confidential balance reads it with a viewing key. Nobody else can. The ring hides how much is held and how much moves. It does not hide which wallets take part.

launching

Connect a wallet, open the launch page, fill in the token details, and pick how the creator allocation and first buy are held. The launch sheet shows exactly what will be public and what will be encrypted before you sign.

Launching is one approval. The token is created, Privacy Rings support is enabled, and the settings are recorded with it permanently.

confidential balances

Any holder can deposit tokens from their public wallet into a confidential balance. The deposit is public, including its amount. After that, the balance is encrypted and changes to it are not readable on chain.

Withdrawing moves tokens back to the public wallet. The withdrawal and its amount are public again.

private transfers

Two wallets with confidential balances can send tokens to each other directly. The sender and recipient are visible. The amount is encrypted, and so is each side’s balance afterwards. Private transfers do not touch the market, so they do not move the price.

A private transfer hides its size best when it comes from a balance that was funded earlier, or funded with more than the transfer. A deposit and a transfer of the same size a moment apart are easy to connect.

creator allocations

A creator can reserve up to 5 percent of supply at launch and choose to hold it in a confidential balance. Its starting size is public because it is deposited at launch. Once the creator moves any of it, what remains is no longer readable.

This gives creators the same discretion as every other holder. It is not a way to hide that an allocation exists.

what stays public

Trades on the market are public. Wallet addresses are public. Deposits into and withdrawals out of confidential balances are public, with amounts. Total supply is public.

What is encrypted: confidential balances, and the amount of each private transfer.

reading the numbers

Value is the market price times supply, in dollars. Private share is total public deposits minus total public withdrawals, divided by supply. Private transfers is a count of events, never an amount.

value          = price_usd * total_supply
private share  = (deposits - withdrawals) / total_supply
private tx 24h = count(private_transfer events, last 24h)
holders        = wallets with a public balance > 0
               + wallets with a confidential balance account

Where a number does not exist yet, LOOP leaves its place empty instead of estimating.

questions

Can I buy a LOOP token without using privacy features?Yes. Buy, sell and hold it publicly like any token. Confidential balances are there when you want them. Can anyone see how much I hold privately?They can see that your wallet has a confidential balance. They cannot read it. Is my wallet address hidden?No. The rings LOOP uses hide amounts and balances, not who sends or receives. Can a creator change privacy settings later?No. They are fixed at launch. Can I leave the confidential balance?Yes, at any time. A withdrawal makes those tokens public again.
